Prototype notes

This is layout + hover fidelity. No real AudioContext — interactions are faked to validate proportions, shared ruler, and drag affordances before builders wire stores.

What this validates

  • [Animation|Audio] tabs inside TimelinePanel.tsx:36 share timelineViewStore ruler/zoom/scroll (no fork)
  • Prompter strip 36–44px + 3 fixed lanes (ROW_HEIGHT each) · 0..slide.duration per-slide
  • Shared playhead spans Prompter+lanes (timeline-playhead absolute)
  • Asset filter chips [All|Images|Audio] + waveform+duration badge
  • Lane-targeted drag MIME-checked, ghost sized assetDuration * pps, snap grid/part
  • Overflow clipped-with-indicator past slide.duration
  • audioClipSelectionStore (not timelineSelectionStore) · trim handles, context menu

Try

  • Switch Animation ↔ Audio tabs
  • Hover/scrub ruler or lanes → playhead follows (fake currentTime)
  • Click a chip/clip → selection highlight
  • Drag audio asset onto a lane → ghost → drop creates clip (try image → rejected toast)
  • Press ● Record → modal with live level + waveform
  • ▶ Play animates playhead 0→duration (fake rAF)

Recording modal contract (from #224)

PrompterPart → Record → getUserMedia + AnalyserNode level → MediaRecorder→WAV base64 → Voice AudioClip at part.start · mismatch >max(0.3s,5%) offers [Speed up/Extend/Discard] or [Slow down/Keep shorter/Discard] with opt-in [Move following parts+clips].

Out of scope for this prototype

Real decodeAudioData, FFmpeg rubberband, TTS, export mix, keyboard a11y — see Not-yet-specified on map #218.
